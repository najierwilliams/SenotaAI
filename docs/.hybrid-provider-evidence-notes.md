# Hybrid Provider Evidence Notes

## Julich-Brain v3.1 / EBRAINS

The official EBRAINS record identifies **Julich-Brain Atlas, cytoarchitectonic maps v3.1**, DOI `10.25493/KNSN-XB4`, released 2024-06-23 under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. It reports 207 maps at default granularity and 227 at high granularity; probability maps, gap maps, and maximum-probability maps are offered in MNI ICBM 152 2009c nonlinear asymmetric, Colin27, and fsaverage. Its surface model covers 148 cortical areas. Source: https://search.kg.ebrains.eu/instances/f1fe19e8-99bd-44bc-9616-a52850680777

The official Julich atlas describes probabilistic cytoarchitectonic maps, a 20 µm BigBrain reference, and receptor/molecular contexts as elements of a multiscale atlas. Its public interfaces include siibra-explorer, siibra-python, and EBRAINS/siibra services. Source: https://julich-brain-atlas.de/atlas

## BigBrain boundary

The BigBrain project’s Julich overview describes Julich probability maps as MNI-reference-space resources and treats BigBrain as linked multiscale context. This does not identify a Luna correspondence. Source: https://bigbrainproject.org/featured-paper-1.html

## Allen Human Brain Atlas boundary

The official Allen API documentation states that human microarray sampling sites are associated with anatomist-assigned structures and mapped to each donor’s MR space; all donor brains are registered to MNI for cross-individual comparisons. Sample records include donor, well/polygon, and MR-volume `(x,y,z)` coordinates in millimetres. These are donor/sample data, not Luna coordinates. The documentation describes an `Alignment3d` MR-to-MNI transform for Allen donor data. Source: https://brain-map.org/support/documentation/human-brain-atlas-api

## Architectural consequence

Julich remains the primary MNI 2009c probabilistic atlas. BigBrain remains provider-scoped linked context. Allen molecular data can be represented as donor/sample-scoped observations and may preserve documented donor-MR/MNI provenance, but it cannot supply a Luna mesh coordinate or automatic Luna-structure mapping. No raw large datasets are required by this metadata architecture.
